NeuroNest is built for the moment when you know what to do, but cannot begin. Instead of asking you to build another perfect system, Rescue starts with the task already on your mind. Type it in. Name the friction: too big, unclear, boring, or hard to do well. NeuroNest turns that context into one small, realistic next action. Start there. If the step still feels wrong, make it smaller or choose a different step. The task stays with you, and the actions you tried stay connected. Rescue works before signup, with anonymous continuity and a clear path from stuck to started. NeuroNest. One small next action. Then begin.
