# NeuroNest Logo Direction

## Brand idea

NeuroNest is the place where a stuck task becomes a safe, small beginning.

The logo should communicate three ideas quickly:

1. **Nest:** safety, containment, and a place to return to.
2. **Neural movement:** thought becoming action without looking clinical.
3. **One small step:** a clear forward cue rather than a complicated brain illustration.

## Recommended visual concept

Use a compact rounded-square mark containing three soft, nested arcs. The arcs feel like a nest and subtly form the rhythm of an “N.” Add one small forward step/notch in warm yellow at the lower right.

The icon should feel:

- Calm, not childish.
- Intelligent, not clinical.
- Memorable at 16px.
- Warm enough for a person who feels stuck.
- Clear enough that someone can describe it as “the purple nest with the little step.”

## Color direction

- Deep background: `#020617`.
- Primary violet: `#8B5CF6`.
- Deep violet: `#4C1D95`.
- Soft lavender line: `#DDD6FE`.
- Step/spark accent: `#FDE68A`.

Use the yellow only as a small point of movement. Do not make the logo a rainbow or add multiple unrelated symbols.

## Primary image-generation prompt

```text
Design a distinctive modern app logo for a productivity product named “NeuroNest”. The product helps people who know what they need to do but cannot begin by giving them one small realistic next action.

Create a simple, highly recognizable icon: three smooth nested arcs forming a protected nest and subtly suggesting the letter N, with one tiny forward step or open notch at the lower-right edge. The nest should communicate safety and containment; the small step should communicate movement from stuck to started. Use a deep midnight navy background, rich violet-to-purple gradient, soft white/lavender linework, and one restrained warm yellow accent for the step.

Style: premium SaaS identity, calm, intelligent, emotionally warm, minimal geometric vector, rounded corners, strong silhouette, no text inside the icon, no literal brain, no neuron diagram, no medical imagery, no motivational cliché, no gradients that reduce legibility, no 3D mockup, no shadows that disappear at small sizes. Centered icon, flat vector, transparent background version also included, suitable for favicon, mobile app icon, website header, LinkedIn avatar, and Instagram profile image.
```

## Wordmark prompt

```text
Create a clean wordmark for “NeuroNest” to pair with a simple nest-and-forward-step app icon. Use a friendly modern sans-serif with slightly rounded terminals, confident lowercase-friendly proportions, and excellent readability. The wordmark should feel calm, capable, and human — not medical, childish, futuristic, or corporate. Use white or very light lavender on dark navy, with a restrained violet accent. Provide horizontal lockup, icon-only lockup, monochrome black, monochrome white, and a one-color embroidery-safe version. No tagline in the logo artwork.
```

## Export checklist

Ask for:

- SVG icon with transparent background.
- SVG horizontal logo lockup.
- 1024px PNG with transparent background.
- 512px and 192px app icons.
- 32px and 16px favicon exports.
- White-only and black-only versions.
- Safe-area version with 20% internal padding.
- Social avatar crop.
- 1200×630 social preview artwork.

## Human test

Show the icon to five people for three seconds and ask:

1. What do you think this product helps with?
2. What do you remember about the shape?
3. Can you draw or describe it afterward?

If people only say “brain app,” remove any remaining neural-looking details. The desired recall is: **nest + small step + calm start**.
